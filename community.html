Backroad Boots UK

Backroad Boots UK champions line dancing across the UK by celebrating real dancers, real socials and the people helping the community grow. Their pages make it easier to discover events, share dance-floor memories and support organisers across the country scene.

Proud to support people helping UK line dancing feel connected, visible and welcoming.
