Meet your instructor

Hi, I'm Nora.

Healthcare professional · Line dance instructor · Country music lover

Where country music brings people together.

My story

Dancing has always been part of who I am.

I've been dancing for as long as I can remember, with 12 years of Ballroom and Latin experience, as well as Street Dance training. Movement, music and bringing people together have always been a huge part of my life.

By day, I work as a healthcare professional, so creating a safe, welcoming and inclusive environment comes naturally to me. Whether it's your very first class or you've been line dancing for years, my aim is to make everyone feel comfortable from the moment they walk through the door.

Country music and line dancing found me again at a time when I needed something that brought me joy. After someone encouraged me to start teaching, I decided to take the leap — and I'm so glad I did.

Today, Boot Scootin' Line Dancing is about much more than learning steps. It's about confidence, laughter, friendships and creating a place where people can switch off for an hour and simply enjoy themselves.

Whether you come on your own or with friends, you'll always be welcomed onto the dance floor.

So grab your boots, bring your smile, and let's dance! 🤠
