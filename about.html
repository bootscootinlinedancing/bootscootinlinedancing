Meet Nora

Hi, I'm Nora.

Founder of Boot Scootin’ Line Dancing Birmingham · Operating Department Practitioner · Country music lover

Where country music brings people together.

The woman behind the boots

A little about me.

I've been dancing for as long as I can remember, with 12 years of Ballroom and Latin experience as well as Street Dance training. Music and movement have always been a huge part of who I am.

Alongside Boot Scootin’, I work in a hospital as a registered Operating Department Practitioner, specialising in anaesthetics within operating theatres.

Working in theatres has taught me to stay calm, communicate clearly, pay attention to safety and make people feel supported. Those are the same qualities I bring into every class.

I'm also a mum, a country-music lover and the Brummie Cowgirl. Outside class, you'll usually find me at a concert, planning a festival outfit, learning a new dance or bringing country fans together.

My favourite part of teaching is watching someone arrive feeling nervous and leave smiling because they achieved something they did not think they could do.

Boot Scootin’ is not just about learning dances. It is about creating a place where people feel welcome, make friends, build confidence and leave smiling. If I can help someone forget about life’s worries for an hour while dancing to great country music, then I have done exactly what I hoped to do.

Professional background

Care, safety and confidence.

  • BSc (Hons) Operating Department Practice
  • Registered Operating Department Practitioner
  • Intermediate Life Support (ILS)
  • First Aid at Work
  • Safeguarding Adults — Level 3
  • Safeguarding Children — Level 3
  • Health and Safety training
  • Equality and Diversity training

These qualifications and training support the calm, inclusive and safety-conscious environment I want people to experience at Boot Scootin’.

Why Boot Scootin’ Exists

More than learning steps.

Country music and line dancing found me again at a time when I needed something that brought me joy. After being encouraged to teach, I took the leap and created Boot Scootin’ Line Dancing in Birmingham.

I wanted to create the kind of place where people could arrive on their own and leave feeling as though they had found their people. Somewhere complete beginners would never feel embarrassed. Somewhere cowboy boots and trainers were equally welcome, and laughter mattered more than getting every step perfect.

Boot Scootin’ grew around a simple belief: people learn best when they feel safe, supported and free to enjoy themselves. The classes may be beginner-friendly, but the heart of Boot Scootin’ is the confidence, friendships and memories that grow alongside the dancing.

It is not about being the best dancer in the room. It is about taking that first step, celebrating the little victories and finding a place where you feel welcome.

Come for the dancing. Stay because you belong.

The Boot Scootin’ Promise

You will always have a place on our dance floor.

Whether you come alone or with friends, whether you wear cowboy boots or trainers, and whether it is your first class or your fiftieth, you will be welcomed with kindness.

We will learn step by step, laugh when things go wrong and celebrate every bit of progress.

What matters here

My Values

The values that shape every class, event and part of the Boot Scootin’ community.

Everyone is welcome

You do not need experience, a partner or a particular look to belong.

No partner needed

Come alone, come with friends or meet new people on the dance floor.

Dance at your own pace

Progress is personal. There is no pressure to keep up with anyone else.

Community over competition

We support one another, celebrate the little wins and enjoy the journey together.

Confidence grows one step at a time

Every attempt counts, even when the steps do not go exactly to plan.

Leave smiling

The most important result is that you had fun and felt good being there.
