Meet your instructor

Hi, I'm Nora.

I started Boot Scootin' Line Dancing to bring people together through a love of country music, movement and genuinely good times.

Classes are relaxed, welcoming and designed to help you learn at your own pace — whether you're stepping onto the dance floor for the first time or already know your vines from your shuffles.
