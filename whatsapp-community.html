Stay connected

Boot Scootin’ WhatsApp Community

A private space for the people who dance with us, support Boot Scootin’ and want to stay connected beyond class night.

What it’s for

More than class updates.

Class updates & reminders

Useful reminders about upcoming classes, venue information, changes and anything you need to know before you arrive.

Country events & socials

Share plans for country nights, festivals, concerts and social get-togethers with people who enjoy the same things.

Dancing & community

Talk about dances, music, class moments and keep in touch with the people you meet on the dance floor.

A friendly members’ space

The aim is simple: good people, useful information, support and a welcoming place to stay connected.

How to join

The joining link is not posted publicly.

To keep the community connected to Boot Scootin’, the WhatsApp joining link is shared with people who come to our classes or join as a member through the website.

If you are new, you do not need to join the WhatsApp community before your first class. Simply book your class and come along — once you are part of Boot Scootin’, you can be given access.
