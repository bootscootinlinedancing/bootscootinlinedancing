Who’s Going?

Members will be able to opt in to show that they are attending a class. The public view will use first name and a chosen profile name only — never private contact details.

Privacy first

Your Trail belongs to you.

No public leaderboard

Progress is personal. There is no ranking of members against one another.

Optional sharing

Members decide whether attendance, badges or profile details are visible to others.

Minimum information

Only the information needed for bookings, rewards and the member’s chosen features will be stored.
