Would you like to apply?

Moonshine & Good Times is a private community. Creating a Boot Scootin’ website account will not automatically provide access.

Members will need to choose to apply, agree to the community rules and wait for Nora to personally approve the application.

1Choose to apply
2Tell Nora why you would like to join
3Agree to the community rules
4Wait for approval

After approval

Your Lounge access unlocks

  • Community announcements
  • Meetups and country-event plans
  • Festival information
  • Approved members-only updates
  • A private invitation to the relevant community space

Approval can be refused or withdrawn to protect the safety, privacy and atmosphere of the community.
