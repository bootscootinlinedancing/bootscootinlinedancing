Class booking

Cancellation and refund summary

  • 48 hours or more: full refund to the original payment method, or a class credit.
  • 24–48 hours: one class transfer or class credit.
  • Less than 24 hours or a no-show: normally no refund or credit, unless the place is resold or exceptional circumstances are agreed.
  • If Boot Scootin’ cancels: full refund or transfer to another class.

Refunds and charges will always be applied fairly and proportionately. Your statutory rights are not affected.

Payment details are entered only on SumUp’s secure hosted checkout. Boot Scootin’ does not collect or store your card details.

Plain and fair terms

Class Cancellation & Refund Policy

Please read this before booking. The summary is also shown again at checkout.

48+ hours’ notice

Choose a full refund to the original payment method or move the value to a future standard class.

24–48 hours’ notice

Choose one transfer to another available standard class or receive class credit. A cash refund is not normally offered at this stage.

Under 24 hours

No refund or credit is normally available because the reserved space may be difficult to refill. If the place is resold, the payment will be refunded.

No-shows

No refund or class credit is normally available. Exceptional circumstances can be considered individually.

Boot Scootin’ cancels

You can choose a full refund or transfer the full value to another class. This also applies where the agreed service cannot be provided.

How to cancel

Use the secure manage-booking link supplied after booking, or email bookings@bootscootinlinedancing.co.uk with your booking reference.

Fairness promise

Any amount retained will reflect the genuine loss caused by the cancellation and reasonable steps will be taken to refill the space. Nothing in this policy limits your statutory consumer rights.

New to line dancing?

Every class is beginner friendly, no partner is needed, and you can learn at your own pace.

What to wear

Comfortable clothing and secure footwear are perfect. Cowboy boots are welcome but never required.

Need help?

Contact Nora before booking if you have a question about access, venues or class suitability.

Before you arrive

Come comfortable and ready to dance.

  • Wear what feels comfortable. Cowboy boots, trainers or other secure comfortable footwear are all absolutely fine.
  • Bring plenty of water. We will definitely work up a sweat!
  • No partner is needed. Complete beginners and solo dancers are always welcome.
  • Arrive around 10 minutes early. This gives us time to check everyone in and start together.

Extra information for a particular class — such as parking changes, a theme or an earlier finish — will appear on that class card as an optional note.
