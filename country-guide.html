A Boot Scootin’ Journey

Buckin’ Boots Summer Stomp 2026

One unforgettable weekend that changed my confidence, my teaching and my line dancing journey.

18–19 July 2026

A weekend I’ll never forget

Summer Stomp brought together dancers from across the UK for workshops, social dancing and a weekend full of encouragement, energy and community. It became one of the most inspiring experiences of my line dancing journey.

Meeting my idols

Troy & Elise — Dirt Road Dancing

For around 18 months, I learned dance after dance from Troy and Elise’s YouTube videos in my living room.

Meeting them, learning from them and dancing alongside them was one of those moments that honestly did not feel real. Their warmth, clarity and passion for building confidence have had a huge influence on my own journey.

“Sometimes the people who inspire you from a screen become memories you’ll treasure for a lifetime.”
Visit Dirt Road Dancing Watch on YouTube

What I brought home

How the weekend will shape my classes

I did not just come home with new dances. I came home with fresh ideas about how to help people learn, relax and believe in themselves.

01

Clearer breakdowns

Breaking dances into smaller, manageable sections so every dancer can understand the movement before adding speed.

02

Confidence before perfection

Giving dancers permission to practise, make variations and build confidence without feeling rushed or judged.

03

Purposeful repetition

Repeating the parts that need it while keeping the class positive, musical and enjoyable.

04

Community first

Creating the same welcoming feeling I experienced: nobody left behind, plenty of encouragement and lots of laughter.

With love and gratitude

Thank you

Thank you to Troy and Elise for being such an inspiration, to Buckin’ Boots and Sovereign Quarter Horses for creating such a brilliant weekend, to Backroad Boots UK for capturing unforgettable moments, and to every dancer who made the experience so special.

I will carry what I learned into every Boot Scootin’ class I teach.
