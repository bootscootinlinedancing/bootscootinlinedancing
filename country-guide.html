My Inspiration

Dirt Road Dancing

Before Nora taught classes of her own, she spent months learning dance after dance from Troy and Elise through their YouTube videos.

Meeting them and dancing alongside them turned something she had imagined from her living room into a real memory. Their passion, teaching and worldwide dance community are an important part of Nora’s own Boot Scootin’ story.

“Sometimes the people who inspire you from a screen become memories you’ll treasure for a lifetime.”
