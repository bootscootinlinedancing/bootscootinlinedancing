Security & Payments

This page explains the current Boot Scootin’ Line Dancing approach. For questions, email hello@bootscootinlinedancing.co.uk.

Payments

Payments are handled through the payment services connected to the booking system. Boot Scootin’ does not intentionally store full card details on this website.

Account protection

Private booking and member information should be accessed only through authorised links or secure sign-in tools.

Reporting a concern

Please report suspicious activity or security concerns to hello@bootscootinlinedancing.co.uk.
