Moonshine&Good Times Gang

A welcoming UK community for country music fans, festival friends and people who simply want more good times with good people.

How it started

A place to find your people.

Nora created Moonshine & Good Times to bring country fans together beyond the dance floor. What began as a way to connect people around events and music has grown into a friendly community filled with conversations, meet-ups, outfit ideas, concert plans and genuine friendships.

Come for the country music. Stay for the people.

Request community access

Help us keep the gang safe.

Moonshine & Good Times Gang is a friendly, carefully managed community. The WhatsApp invitation link is never shared publicly.

Before receiving an invitation, please introduce yourself and provide a social-media profile so Nora can review each request personally.

Why do we do this?

A short approval process helps protect members from spam, fake accounts and people joining for the wrong reasons. It keeps the community welcoming, respectful and safe for everyone.
