How it started

A place to find your people.

Nora created Moonshine & Good Times to bring country fans together beyond the dance floor. What began as a way to connect people around events and music has grown into a friendly community filled with conversations, meet-ups, outfit ideas, concert plans and genuine friendships.

Come for the country music. Stay for the people.
