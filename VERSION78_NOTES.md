# Version 78 — Member Hub Preview

## Added
- One connected, app-style member area at `member-hub.html`.
- Internal navigation between Dashboard, My Trail, My Passport, My Journey, My Bookings, Dance Library, Trail Outfitters, Moonshine Lounge and My Profile.
- Responsive desktop sidebar and mobile section selector.
- Random welcoming messages on the dashboard.
- Member preview cards for rank, points, streak, bookings and achievements.
- Public Trail page now links into the complete Member Hub preview.
- Member Hub Preview added to the main website menu.

## Important
- This is a visual and structural preview only.
- No real member account is created.
- No password, personal member data, points or reward redemptions are active.
- Authentication, email verification, privacy rules and Row Level Security must be connected before member launch.
