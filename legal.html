Important information about using the website, bookings and your privacy.

Terms & Conditions

Bookings are for the class, date and number of places selected at checkout. Please provide accurate contact details so we can send confirmations or important class updates. Participation is at your own pace and you should only take part in movements you feel comfortable performing.

Cancellations & Refunds

Cancellation and refund arrangements shown during booking form part of your booking. If Boot Scootin’ cancels a class, affected customers will be offered the appropriate refund, credit or alternative in line with the booking information provided.

Privacy

Personal information supplied for bookings, enquiries, rewards or member features is used only to provide those services, communicate with you and administer Boot Scootin’ Line Dancing. We do not sell customer personal information.

Cookies & Website Data

The website may use essential browser storage or cookies needed for functions such as sessions, preferences, bookings and secure account features. Optional third-party services may have their own privacy terms.

Accessibility

We aim to make the website and classes welcoming and accessible. If you need information in another format or have an access requirement for a class, please contact us before attending so we can help where reasonably possible.
