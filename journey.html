Account deletion process

  1. Confirm using a password or secure email link.
  2. Sharing with Nora stops immediately.
  3. All active sessions are signed out.
  4. A seven-day cancellation period begins.
  5. Profile, photo, notes and progress are permanently removed.
🔒

🛡️

👁️

💳

No card storage

Account Settings

Members stay in control.

Download my data

Export dances, confidence ratings, notes and milestones.

Pause my account

Hide the profile, stop sharing and sign out while keeping progress for later.

Delete my account

Permanently remove the profile, dance progress and notes after secure confirmation and a short cancellation period.

Deletion preview

Before an account is permanently deleted:

  1. The member confirms by password or secure email link.
  2. Sharing with Nora stops immediately.
  3. All active sessions are revoked.
  4. A seven-day cancellation period begins.
  5. Profile, progress and notes are permanently removed.

Mailing lists, WhatsApp, Eventbrite and SumUp remain separate and must be managed independently.
