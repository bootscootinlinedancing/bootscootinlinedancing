Deletion preview

Before an account is permanently deleted:

  1. The member confirms by password or secure email link.
  2. Sharing with Nora stops immediately.
  3. All active sessions are revoked.
  4. A seven-day cancellation period begins.
  5. Profile, progress and notes are permanently removed.

Mailing lists, WhatsApp, Eventbrite and SumUp remain separate and must be managed independently.
